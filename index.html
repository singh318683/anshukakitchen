Welcome to Anshu Ka Kitchen

A bilingual home-cooking companion for Indian food.

Indian cooking is often passed down by watching, not writing — a pinch of this, a "cook until it smells right." That's wonderful in a family kitchen, but hard to follow when you're starting out on your own. This app turns that oral tradition into something you can actually follow: real ingredient amounts, real timers, and steps written the way an experienced home cook would explain them to you in person.
